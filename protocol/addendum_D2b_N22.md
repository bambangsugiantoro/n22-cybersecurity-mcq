# Addendum D2b - N22 (post-hoc, written 16 Nov 2026 17:17 WIB, after seeing the D2 model-free baseline for two models)
The "choose the longest option" baseline reached 0.406 on CyberMetric-2000, close to question-free accuracy. To separate an option-length artifact from other option-only signal, script n22_post2.py (CPU only, existing prediction files, no new model run) reports, per model on CyberMetric-2000: accuracy of the longest-option rule; question-free accuracy (published order, registered prompt) on items where the longest option is correct and where it is not, with bootstrap 95% interval (4000, seed 2211); full-item accuracy under R0 on the same two subsets; the share of question-free correct answers that coincide with the longest option being correct. No H1-H4 rule changes. Exploratory.
